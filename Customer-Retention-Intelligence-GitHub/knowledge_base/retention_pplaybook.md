# Telecom Customer Retention Playbook
## Purpose 
This playbook provides practical areaas for a retention team to review when a 
customer has an elevated model-predicted churn risk.
The suggestions are decision-support ideas. They are not causal conclusions
from the churn model.
## 1. High Churn Risk
When a customer has a high model-predicted churn probability:
1. Review the customer's account profile.
2. Review contract type and tenure.
3. Review service configuration.
4. Review monthly charges.
5. Review payment method.
6. Check whether technical-support or service-related issues are visible in 
the customer profile.
7. Use the model explanation to identify the features that contributed most to 
the prediction.
The purpose is to prioritize customers for human review.
## 2. Month-to-Month Customers
Customers with month-to-month contracts can be reviewed for:
- Contract exprerience 
- Pricing concerns
- Service satisfaction
- Available long-term plans
- Relevant account benefits
A longer contract should not automatically be presented as the correct action.
The customer's circumstances should be considered.
## 3. Short-Tenure Customers
Customers with short tenure can be reviewed for:
- Early onboarding experience
- Service setup issues
- Technical problems
- Understanding of the selected plan
- Support experience
The model may identify short-tenure customers as high-risk, but the reason
for the risk should be checked using the customer's other information and model explanation.
## 4. Higher Monthly Charges 
When monthly charges are relatively high, a retention review can include:
- Current plan 
- Service included in the plan
- Optional Services 
- Customer usage 
- Available plan
The model's prediction should not be interpreted as evidence that price alone
caused the customer's churn risk.
## 5. Service and Support Review
For customers with elevated risk, the retention team can review:
- Internet Service
- Online Security
- Online backup 
- Device protection
- Technical Support 
- Streaming services
This is a review framework,not a causal conclusion
## 6. Payment Method Review
Payment method can be included in account review when the model identifies
it as an important features for a customer.
The purpose is to check whether there are payment difficulties , failed payments,
or customer preferences that need attention.
## 7. Customer-Level Review
For an individual customer, the recommended workflow is:
Customer
- Churn Propability
- Top model contributors
- Account reiew
- Appropriate retention conversation
The model should support the employee's decision rather than replace it.
## 8. Improtant Limitation 
No retention action in this document should be described as guaranteed to reduce
churn.
The current project does not contain randomized intervention data, so it 
cannot measure the causal effect of individual retention actions
